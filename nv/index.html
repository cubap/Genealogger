The nv: namespace holds the Eventities-specific terms of the Genealogger model — event subtypes, participation roles, confidence, lacunae, and the derived parent/child links. Everything else reuses a standard vocabulary: schema.org for facts about people, Web Annotation for assertions, DCTERMS for records and dates, PROV-O, WGS84 and OWL Time for places and dates.

The normative machine-readable definition is schemas/genealogger-context.jsonld. The words it uses are fixed by GLOSSARY.md.
