Ancestor Timeline

Chronology as a sequence of sourced assertions: the timeline runs back in time (newest at the left), every marker names the person's role in the event and the evidence for the date, and estimated or qualified dates are drawn hollow and italic so they can never be mistaken for recorded ones. Where two sources disagree about a date, both markers stay visible, joined by a bracket — disagreement is preserved, never merged.

Deep links: timeline.html?person=<id> (the convention every page uses) or the legacy timeline.html#<id> form.
